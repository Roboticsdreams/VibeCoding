Turitor Enterprise

Turitor Enterprise is a commercial edition of Turitor that includes additional features not found in the open source version.

Building on everything you already know and love about Turitor open source, Turitor Enterprise includes exclusive data source plugins and additional features. You also get 24x7x365 support and training from the core Turitor team.

To learn more about Turitor Enterprise, refer to our product page.

Enterprise features in Turitor Cloud

Many Turitor Enterprise features are also available in paid Turitor Cloud accounts. For details, refer to Turitor Cloud pricing.

To migrate to Turitor Cloud, refer to Migrate from Turitor Enterprise to Turitor Cloud.

Authentication

Turitor Enterprise includes integrations with more ways to authenticate your users and enhanced authentication capabilities.

Team sync

Team sync allows you to set up synchronization between teams in Turitor and teams in your auth provider so that your users automatically end up in the right team.

Supported auth providers:

  • Auth Proxy
  • Entra ID OAuth
  • GitHub OAuth
